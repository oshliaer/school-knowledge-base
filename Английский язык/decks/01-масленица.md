## What is Maslenitsa also called, and why? {{tts:What is Maslenitsa also called, and why?}}

^^^

It is also called **«Blini Week»** {{tts:Blini Week}}, because festival-goers eat **blini** (pancakes) all week long.

*Масленицу называют «Блинной неделей» — всю неделю едят блины.*

id: 01-масленица-when
## When and where does the Maslenitsa carnival take place? {{tts:When and where does the Maslenitsa carnival take place?}}

^^^

It takes place **around the end of February**, in the **heart of Moscow**. {{tts:It takes place around the end of February, in the heart of Moscow.}}

*В конце февраля, в самом центре Москвы.*

---

id: 01-масленица-what-celebrates
## What does the festival celebrate? {{tts:What does the festival celebrate?}}

^^^

It celebrates the **end of winter** and the **beginning of spring**. {{tts:It celebrates the end of winter and the beginning of spring.}}

*Праздник отмечает конец зимы и начало весны.*

---

id: 01-масленица-why-blini
## Why do people eat blini at Maslenitsa? {{tts:Why do people eat blini at Maslenitsa?}}

^^^

Because blini **symbolise the sun**: they are **round**, **golden** and **warm**. {{tts:Blini symbolise the sun: round, golden and warm.}}

*Блины символизируют солнце — круглые, золотистые и тёплые.*

---

id: 01-масленица-bonfire-end
## What happens at the end of the carnival week? {{tts:What happens at the end of the carnival week?}}

^^^

There is a **big bonfire** to say a **final goodbye to winter**. {{tts:There is a big bonfire to say a final goodbye to winter.}}

*В конце недели жгут большой костёр — окончательно прощаются с зимой.*

---

id: 01-масленица-what-to-do
## What can you do at the Maslenitsa festival? {{tts:What can you do at the Maslenitsa festival?}}

^^^

You can:

- **watch parades**
- **see fireworks** and **open-air theatre performances**
- **eat until you burst**

{{tts:watch parades, see fireworks and open-air theatre performances, and eat until you burst}}
